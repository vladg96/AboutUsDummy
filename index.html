About Us

Founded in 2010 and based in Nairobi, Kenya, EcoHealth Research Institute (EHRI) is a leading nonprofit organization dedicated to enhancing public health systems across sub-Saharan Africa. Our work bridges the gap between environmental data, epidemiology, and digital innovation.

We collaborate with international partners and local governments to design and implement scalable health interventions. Our current focus areas include infectious disease modeling, climate-resilient health infrastructure, and AI-powered early warning systems.

Our Mission

To empower communities and health systems with cutting-edge research and practical solutions for a healthier, climate-resilient future.
